# Multiplayer catalog sources

Checked October 30, 2026. This is a launch inventory, not live in-game validation.

- [Activision launch guide, October 19](https://www.callofduty.com/uk/en/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-multiplayer-operators-weapons-equipment-create-a-class): weapon names, categories, player unlock levels, weapon levels, base rounds, advertised fire rate, Apex descriptions and explicitly blocked slots; equipment, perk slots, levels and descriptions. The guide says full weapon leveling unlocks each supported Apex. It also provides the official weapon/Apex comparison images.
- [Infinity Ward launch patch notes, updated October 29](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes): October 28 Multiplayer tuning and Quick Fix correction; separate DMZ tuning; UAV five-kill launch cost or four with Hardline. The October 19 guide says four. The patch notes state that the second beta weekend increased all streak costs, but give no other exact current costs. We retain unknowns rather than apply an assumed increment.
- [SenseOS streak source analysis](https://senseos.gg/guides/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4/killstreaks/): reviews the contradictory sources and notes that the in-game menu is needed to establish costs. A [different guide](https://mw4.vi.ki/killstreaks) extrapolates an additional kill from the guide values. Those are estimates, not independent measurements.
- [PC Gamer launch review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-review/) reports five standard attachments. The official launch guide states Apex uses a separate slot. This does not establish every compatibility rule.

The guide lists 24 primary weapons, eight secondary weapons and one dedicated melee weapon, plus nine tacticals, 11 lethals, 12 field upgrades, 18 perks and 17 streaks. All records have unique IDs. Player levels are unlock milestones, while weapon maximum levels apply to weapon progression and Apex unlocks. Weapon RPM and magazine values come from the launch guide, not measured current multiplayer; current patch effects and any Apex changes are not applied to them.

The guide describes field recharge as fast, medium or slow and supplies no seconds. It does not provide enough current mode-specific damage, timing, projectile, accuracy, recoil, attachment or stacking data for numeric optimization. Unavailable values stay unknown. Blocked Apex slots reproduce the slots explicitly stated in the guide; an empty list is not evidence that all combinations are compatible. Weapon and gear role scores are editorial fit estimates, with a neutral baseline of 45 and purpose-specific adjustments. Apex scores are signed editorial adjustments. They are not observed game statistics or claims of measured performance.

The guide's image next to Cronen Squall depicts a streak chart and the image next to PM-40 Anvil depicts a different weapon. Neither is assigned to those records. Other linked images depict the named weapon or its official base/Apex composite. Treat those composites as comparisons, not isolated attachment photos.
